Ma CAV Plus subscription

Ma CAV Plus subscriptions are managed by Apple through the App Store. To view, change or cancel your subscription on iPhone:

  1. Open Settings on your iPhone.
  2. Tap your name, then Subscriptions.
  3. Select Ma CAV Plus to see the available options.

Apple’s subscription management guide

Restore your purchases

If your Ma CAV Plus purchase or subscription is not recognized, check that you are using the same Apple Account for the App Store as when you made the purchase, then use the Restore Purchases option in Ma CAV.

Check your internet connection and, for a subscription, its status in your Apple Account settings. If the issue continues, email us with a description of what happens.

Apple’s guide to restoring in-app purchases

iCloud sync

Ma CAV uses iCloud to sync your cellar data. If your data does not appear on another device:

  • Check that your devices use the same Apple Account for iCloud.
  • In iCloud settings, check that Ma CAV is allowed to use iCloud.
  • Check your internet connection and available iCloud storage.
  • Open Ma CAV on the affected devices and allow time for syncing to finish.

If the issue continues, tell us which devices are affected and which data appears to be missing.

Help us help you

In your email, include your device model, iOS version, Ma CAV version and the steps leading to the issue. You can attach a screenshot with personal information hidden.
