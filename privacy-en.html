Ma CAV Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

Ma CAV is an application designed to help users manage their wine cellar, bottles, storage locations and related information.

Ma CAV is designed to minimize the collection and processing of personal data.

1. Data stored in Ma CAV

Information you enter into Ma CAV, including your cellars, bottles, wines, storage locations, meals, preferences and other information related to your use of the application, may be stored and synchronized using Apple's iCloud and CloudKit services.

2. iCloud and CloudKit

For online storage and synchronization of user data, Ma CAV uses only Apple’s iCloud and CloudKit, without any proprietary server.

Ma CAV uses Apple's iCloud and CloudKit technologies to provide data storage and, where available, synchronization between your devices.

This data is associated with your iCloud account and is processed by Apple in accordance with Apple's applicable terms and privacy policies.

Ma CAV does not operate its own server for storing a copy of your wine cellar or its contents.

3. Advertising and profiling

Ma CAV contains no advertising and does not profile users.

Ma CAV does not use the contents of your wine cellar for advertising purposes and does not perform advertising profiling of its users.

Ma CAV does not sell data relating to your use of the application to third parties.

4. Purchases and subscriptions

Purchases and subscriptions offered through Ma CAV are processed by Apple through the App Store.

Ma CAV does not have access to your complete payment information, such as your credit or debit card number.

Technical information relating to the status of purchases or subscriptions may be used by the application to determine which features you are entitled to access.

5. Deleting your data

You can delete data you have created in Ma CAV using the features available within the application.

Certain synchronized data may also be managed through the iCloud settings associated with your Apple Account.

6. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect changes to Ma CAV or its features.

The date of the most recent update is displayed at the top of this page.

7. Contact

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy or Ma CAV, you may contact the developer at:
